For Ananya Maam

Science — and our Science Competition guide
Ananya Maam

Dear Ma'am,


Aapse jitna science seekhne ko mila, utna kisi textbook se nahi mil sakta tha.


Aapne humesha dhairya se samjhaya — chahe practical ho ya theory, har baar naye aur aasan tareeke se concept clear kiya.


Science competition ke liye jitna waqt aur mehnat aapne diya, wo hum kabhi nahi bhoolenge. Har experiment, har presentation ke peeche aapki guidance thi — hum akele wahan tak kabhi nahi pahunch pate.


Is Teachers' Day par, hum sirf itna kehna chahte hain — shukriya, ma'am. Aapka saath aur aapki seekh hamesha yaad rahegi.


— Dibakar Payun
